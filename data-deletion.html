Browser-local saves

These playtest games store progress, guesses, completion history and preferences in your browser, not in a GABAnode game account. Deleting local saves is irreversible; streaks and progress cannot be restored by us.

  1. Close all open copies of the game so an open session does not save old in-memory progress again.
  2. Open your browser's privacy or site-data settings. Locate the actual site hosting the game and remove its stored site data, including local storage. Clearing cached images and files alone is not enough.
  3. Repeat for other browsers, devices or separately hosted editions where you played.
  4. Reopen the game to start fresh. It will create default preferences and new progress as you play.

If both games share a hosting origin, clearing that site's data can reset both games and shared settings. Browser site-data removal may also remove other data belonging to that site. Do not clear all Facebook data indiscriminately.

Games inside Facebook

Facebook runs these games in an embedded hosting context. The hosting site and browser storage partition may differ from facebook.com. Storage controls vary by browser and Facebook client. For help identifying the relevant site-data controls, contact admin@gabanodelab.com with your browser/client and device version. Do not assume removing an app connection or clearing the Facebook page cache deletes all local game saves.

Deleting local saves does not delete information held by Meta, including advertising information processed when using an advertising review edition. Use Meta's account and privacy controls for Meta-held data; consult the Meta Privacy Policy. GABAnode cannot delete another company's account records from your browser.

Restart is not delete-all

Route Relay's Restart clears the current route but retains its hint count. It does not remove your history, preferences or other puzzle records. Closing a tab also does not delete saved progress.

Other information

Local deletion does not erase clipboard history, results you pasted elsewhere or support correspondence. To request deletion of information you sent us, email admin@gabanodelab.com, preferably from the address used for the original correspondence. Describe the information/request; do not send identity documents or passwords. We will review the request, ask only for information needed to identify the relevant correspondence, and explain any legal/security reason that prevents deletion. We cannot remotely erase browser-local saves, third-party account records or results you posted elsewhere. See Support for the information needed to report a problem without sending sensitive data.
