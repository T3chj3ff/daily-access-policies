Privacy notice

Effective September 28, 2026.

This notice describes the current ad-free playtest versions of Word Signal and Route Relay, operated by GABAnode Lab LLC. It does not cover other GABAnode products.

Game progress on your device

Our game code stores progress and preferences in your browser's local storage. Word Signal saves recorded guesses, revealed-clue counts and puzzle results. Route Relay saves your path and hint count. Both use completion dates, game identifiers, practice selections and accessibility preferences to restore play and display your journal and streak. Route Relay also remembers your input mode.

These records have no automatic time-based expiry. Older saved records can remain after an app update. Browser settings or storage eviction can remove them. Our code does not encrypt the records or associate them with a game account. Other people using the same browser storage context may be able to access them. Please do not enter personal or sensitive information as a guess.

Our game code does not upload these saved records to a GABAnode game server. The current games have no app-operated backend, account synchronization, custom analytics, in-game advertising or purchases. Your progress can differ between browsers, devices and separately hosted editions.

Facebook and hosting

The Facebook editions run inside Meta's platform and load the Meta Instant Games SDK. Our integration initializes the game, reports loading progress, starts play and handles supported pause/resume events. The current game code does not request player profile, friend-list or email data.

Meta operates the surrounding platform and may process account, device, network and usage information under its own policies. Our lack of a game backend does not mean Meta receives no information. See the Meta Privacy Policy for its practices and controls.

This policy-only website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub documents that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visitors who are not signed in. GitHub controls its service logging and retention; see its Privacy Statement. These policy pages do not add analytics, advertising, forms or tracking scripts. This hosting disclosure does not describe the separate Facebook game host. Fonts, icons and puzzle content are bundled with the game rather than fetched from a third-party font service.

Optional read-aloud clues

When you select Word Signal's read-aloud control, the revealed, authored clue text is sent to your browser's speech-synthesis service. We do not record microphone audio or send your guesses to that service. Voice processing depends on your browser and chosen voice and is not guaranteed to remain entirely on your device.

Sharing results

Selecting Share result copies a summary to your clipboard, or offers selectable text if copying fails. The game does not read your clipboard or automatically post to Facebook. Depending on the edition and build, results may include a game-page link. Inspect copied text before posting it, and do not share private developer-preview links. Your operating system may retain or synchronize clipboard content according to its settings. You choose where to paste the result.

Support messages and your choices

If you contact us, your email provider and our support provider process the information you choose to send. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. GABAnode monitors the contact below and handles support and privacy/deletion requests there. We delete resolved game-support emails after 90 days unless a legal or security need requires longer retention. This practice applies to our support correspondence, not records controlled separately by Meta, GitHub, your email provider or your browser. Contact admin@gabanodelab.com. Do not send passwords, identity documents, medical information or unredacted account screenshots.

Read the data-deletion instructions for local saves and the limits of local deletion. Requests concerning Meta-held information must use Meta's privacy controls or contact routes. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us, or to ask a privacy question. Applicable rights and exceptions depend on your location and the processing involved. We cannot retrieve browser-only game saves from a GABAnode server.

Audience

These are general-audience games, not directed at children under 13. A puzzle being suitable for different ages does not establish permission to use Facebook or other services outside their age requirements. If you believe a child under 13 sent us personal information, contact us so we can investigate and handle deletion as appropriate.

Changes

Before adding advertising, payments, accounts or analytics, we will review the corresponding disclosures and release requirements and update this notice. The current playtest versions do not contain in-game ads or purchases. An intention to monetize in the future does not mean advertising is currently enabled.
