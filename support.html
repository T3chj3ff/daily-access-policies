Game support

Updated September 28, 2026.

Report a problem

For private playtests, reply through the person who arranged your session. Public support contact: admin@gabanodelab.com.

Useful details include:

  • Word Signal or Route Relay, and whether you played on Facebook or the standalone website.
  • Your browser, device and operating-system versions.
  • The puzzle date or practice number, what you expected, and the steps that produced the problem.
  • Your input method or assistive technology and version, if relevant and you choose to share it.
  • An optional screenshot with account details, private messages and unrelated information removed.

Do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents, medical information or full browser-storage exports.

GABAnode monitors this mailbox for support and privacy/deletion requests. Resolved game-support emails are deleted after 90 days unless a legal or security need requires longer retention. See our Privacy notice for the scope and limitations of this practice.

Accessibility feedback

Accessibility is part of our design and testing process. These early playtests have not completed all assistive-technology or disabled-player testing, and we do not claim accessibility certification. Tell us about barriers, confusing instructions, unexpected focus or announcements, and controls that are difficult to use.

Saved progress

Progress stays in the browser and hosting context where you played. Changing device, clearing site data or using another edition may show different progress. We cannot recover local saves from an app server because the current games do not have one. Read Data deletion before clearing anything.

Private Facebook launch problems

Private test access is limited to approved accounts. Ask the playtest organizer to verify your access and provide the appropriate launch path. A developer preview link is not a public invitation. Do not share account credentials to obtain access.
